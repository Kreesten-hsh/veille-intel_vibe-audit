## 4. Recurring Customer Complaints with Alternatives
1. **Incompréhensible pour un non-technique** : *"The security scanner sent us a 40-page PDF listing CVSS vulnerabilities like 'Missing Content-Security-Policy header' or 'CORS wildcard', but our team has no engineer and we had no idea what to type in Lovable to fix it."*
2. **Le piège de la fausse réassurance de ChatGPT** : *"I pasted my code in ChatGPT and it said everything looked clean. Two weeks later, a user discovered they could query other users' invoices because Row Level Security was never turned on in Supabase."*
3. **Prix disproportionnés pour les micro-SaaS** : *"Every security firm quoted us a minimum engagement of 3,500 €. Our entire app took 3 days to build with Bolt.new, we just couldn't justify paying that."*
