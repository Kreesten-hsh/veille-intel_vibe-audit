# Offers lens
Score: 8/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Massive and growing acute pain**: Non-technical builders are deploying vibe-coded apps into production with real Stripe keys and user databases. They are terrified of leaking data, but traditional penetration tests cost 3,000 € to 10,000 €, which they cannot afford.
- **Copy-Paste Fixes (Near Zero Effort)**: Translating complex security flaws into exact prompts ready to paste into Lovable or Bolt is a killer value proposition. The founder doesn't need to learn cybersecurity; they just click paste.
- **Speed to Value**: A 48-hour delivery timeline directly matches the hyper-fast development velocity of vibe-coders.

## What would kill it
1. **Free AI Substitution Belief**: The founder thinking "I can just ask Claude or ChatGPT to audit my repo for free."
2. **Reluctance to share repo or staging access**: Handing code to an unknown individual without strict nondisclosure agreements or escrow.
3. **Low willingness to pay before first paying users**: If the app has zero revenue, the founder may treat security as an optional future problem.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How do you convince a founder that free AI tools cannot audit database permissions (Supabase RLS) and live endpoints without human inspection?
- What guarantees that you won't disclose their intellectual property or vulnerabilities publicly?
- Can you target founders who have *already* announced their first paying customers?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Pre-pack a Public Redacted Sample**: Publish a sanitized sample audit based on an intentionally vulnerable demo app (`work/demo-vulnerable-app`) so prospects can visualize the exact deliverable.
2. **Clear Guarantee**: "Si aucune faille exploitable ou amélioration concrète n'est identifiée, vous êtes remboursé à 100%."
3. **Formal NDA & Written Consent Form**: Standardize a 1-page bilateral confidentiality and authorization agreement prior to inspection.
