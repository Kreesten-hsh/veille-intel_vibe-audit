# Product lens
Score: 8/10 | Vote: FUND

## What is strong
- **Radical Empathy for the Non-Technical User**: Traditional pentest reports read like alien cryptographic manuals filled with CVSS scores and technical jargon. Vibe-Audit speaks human language: "Impact Business : Un visiteur peut voir les emails de vos clients", suivi de "Voici le prompt à coller dans Lovable pour corriger cela en 2 minutes".
- **Turnaround Speed (48h)**: In the vibe-coding world, waiting 3 weeks for an audit report is an eternity. 48 hours keeps the momentum alive.
- **Craft in the Prompts**: The true product is not the list of bugs; it is the **quality and precision of the remediation prompts**. If the prompt fixes the bug on the first attempt in Bolt/Lovable, the product experience is pure magic.

## What would kill it
1. **Broken Prompts that Hallucinate Fixes**: If the suggested prompt breaks the client's app layout or database migrations, user trust evaporates.
2. **Alarmist Tone**: Terrifying the founder instead of guiding them calmly through remediation.
3. **Overcomplicated Onboarding**: Requiring 10 forms or complex repository access permissions.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How do you verify that your remediation prompt actually works inside Lovable before putting it in the PDF?
- What does the first 60 seconds of reading the report feel like for a non-technical founder?
- Can onboarding happen in less than 3 minutes (URL + simple written consent)?

## If FUND: recommendations
1. **Calm, Constructive Editorial Tone**: Frame findings as "Quick Wins de Sécurité" rather than dramatic failures.
2. **Validation on Live Testbed**: Test every remediation prompt against the project's own vulnerable demo app (`work/demo-vulnerable-app`) before delivery.
3. **Ultra-Simple Onboarding**: 1 simple Google Form / Typeform link with checkboxes for consent, repository link, and staging credentials.
