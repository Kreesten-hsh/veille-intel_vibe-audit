# Board Brief: Veille-Intel

## The Idea
Veille-Intel is a white-label weekly competitive intelligence service sold on a monthly subscription to boutique French B2B agencies (marketing, growth, SEO, content; team size 2 to 20).
Instead of hiring an in-house analyst or paying for expensive enterprise SaaS tools, the agency resells a weekly French intelligence report under its own branding to its retained clients (charging an estimated 600 € - 800 € / month, keeping a ~200 € - 300 € margin).

## Value Proposition & Core Deliverable
- Up to 5 monitored competitors per end-client.
- Delivered every Monday: a 3-to-6 page French PDF report.
- 5 to 10 verified signals per week: pricing & packaging shifts, feature launches, content/messaging shifts, job openings, key public announcements.
- Strict structure: capture date and verified public source, exact factual changes, separated analysis/interpretation, and 3 immediate actionable recommendations.
- Searchable signal archive provided.
- Fixed scope: no live meetings, no ad-hoc bespoke research requests.

## Pricing & Commercial Model
- Intro price: 399 € / month for the first 3 agency clients.
- Standard price: 499 € / month thereafter.
- No free pilots or trials (first month prepaid).
- Payment method: Direct SEPA wire transfer to a European IBAN (Moneco) or African account (Ecobank). No Stripe, PayPal, or automated credit card billing.
- Facturation: International sole proprietor / freelancer invoice without EU VAT.

## Operator & Production Engine
- Solo operator: Kreesten (junior developer based in Abomey-Calavi, Benin, completing a degree in computer systems & software).
- Tools: Existing AI subscriptions (ChatGPT, Gemini Pro, Antigravity) + custom automated scrapers / public web inspection scripts.
- Budget: Exactly 0 € external spend. No paid data feeds, no paid enrichment APIs.
- Strict manual review: all AI-generated extractions are human-verified for accuracy before delivery.

## Target & Timeline
- Hard deadline: December 31, 2026 (~2.5 months).
- Financial threshold floor: 1,000,000 FCFA (~1,524 € / 4 received monthly payments).
- Financial target: 2,000,000 FCFA (~3,049 € / 8 monthly payments).
- Acquisition rhythm: 10 highly targeted, personalized cold outreach emails per day with a customized mini-sample report attached. No automated mass emailing.
- Stop-rule checkpoints: by ~October 24 (~100 outreaches), must achieve at least 5 replies and 2 calls; by ~November 10 (~250 outreaches), must achieve at least 1 closed sale. Otherwise, stop or pivot.
