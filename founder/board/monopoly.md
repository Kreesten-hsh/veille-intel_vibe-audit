# Monopoly lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Starts in a focused micro-niche**: Boutique French B2B agencies (2-20 staff) seeking white-label deliverables represent a clearly bounded initial pool that can be directly mapped and dominated before competitors notice.
- **Service vs. Tool differentiation**: Most competitors are self-serve SaaS platforms (Klue, Crayon, Visualping, SEMrush). Agencies buy these tools and abandon them because nobody has time to triage alerts. A human-verified, "done-for-you" French PDF solves the execution barrier.
- **The insight / secret**: Agencies love billing recurring advisory retainers, but hate doing tedious weekly research. The value is not the raw data (which is public), but the curated synthesis that requires zero agency effort.

## What would kill it
1. **Low technological defensibility (No 10x moat)**: Scraping public websites and summarizing diffs with LLMs is easily replicated by any tech-savvy agency junior or competing freelancer within months.
2. **High operational drag per account**: Manually reviewing 5 competitors x 8 clients = 40 entities every single week. If client volume scales without robust deterministic scrapers, quality drops or the operator burns out.
3. **No network effect or lock-in**: An agency can cancel at any time, copy your exact prompt and report format, and delegate it internally once they see how it is structured.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What proprietary workflow or automated monitoring pipeline prevents you from spending 20 hours a week on manual reporting once you have 5 clients?
- Why wouldn't an agency director simply copy your template and assign it to an intern after month two?
- Can you corner one single vertical first (e.g., exclusively B2B SaaS agencies in Paris) to build a localized mini-monopoly?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Hyper-Verticalize First**: Pitch exclusively one single sub-sector of agencies (e.g. B2B SaaS growth agencies) where competitor moves share similar patterns, accelerating synthesis and domain expertise.
2. **Proprietary Benchmark Database**: Build a cumulative historical archive of tracked changes and benchmarks that becomes increasingly valuable and sticky over time.
3. **Streamlined Pipeline**: Enforce strict automation on public scraping and diffing so that human verification takes under 45 minutes per client report.
