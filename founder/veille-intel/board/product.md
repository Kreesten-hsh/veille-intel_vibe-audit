# Product lens
Score: 7/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Ruthless discipline on scope**: Saying no to custom research, no to live strategy calls, and no to infinite dashboards. The product is strictly ONE thing: a 3-to-6 page French PDF report delivered weekly.
- **Empathetic user journey**: An agency account director wakes up on Monday at 08:00, opens the email, checks 5 high-impact bullet points, affixes their agency banner, and forwards it to their client. The client is impressed; the agency looks brilliant without doing any work.
- **Separation of Fact vs. Interpretation**: Distinguishing verified raw change from strategic insight is a hallmark of high-craft intelligence reporting.

## What would kill it
1. **Shoddy visual aesthetics or automated look**: If the PDF looks like a generic Markdown export, an unstyled Word doc, or an AI dump, the agency will be embarrassed to forward it to their paying clients.
2. **Hallucinated or trivial signals**: If a signal is a typo fix, a minor button color change, or worse, an invented product update, agency credibility with their end-client is instantly destroyed.
3. **Delivery slippage**: Competitive intelligence delivered late on Tuesday afternoon loses 80% of its perceived professional value. Timing must be clockwork.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What does the deliverable look like at first glance? Does the design instantly convey institutional authority and polish?
- What is your editorial filter to discard low-signal noise (e.g. routine CSS fixes, minor copyright updates) from true strategic shifts?
- How do you guarantee delivery every single Monday before 08:30 CET without fail?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Executive Typographic Standard**: The PDF template must be designed with extreme care (clean hierarchy, professional grid, subtle callouts, verified source badges).
2. **Zero-Noise Editorial Bar**: Strict policy: if there are only 3 meaningful signals this week, send 3 high-impact signals rather than padding with trivial noise.
3. **Clockwork SLA**: Monday 08:00 CET hard delivery deadline, every single week.
