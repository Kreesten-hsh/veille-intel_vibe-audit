# Offers lens
Score: 6/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Clear agency upside**: Reselling white-label deliverables allows agencies to increase their monthly client retainer without adding payroll or managing an internal analyst.
- **Low client effort**: The agency receives a finished, branded PDF ready to forward. Time-to-value for the agency is virtually instantaneous once the report drops on Monday morning.
- **Explicit target market**: Boutique B2B marketing, growth, and SEO agencies (2-20 employees) in France are readily identifiable, active online, and live on recurring retainers.

## What would kill it
1. **Unproven perceived likelihood & trust gap**: An unknown individual operator invoicing internationally with no past case studies or testimonials asking for a 399 € prepayment upfront will face severe hesitation.
2. **Thin agency margin**: If the agency resells the service for 600 € to 700 €, keeping only 200 € to 300 € while taking 100% of the client reputational risk is an unfavorable risk/reward equation for them.
3. **Friction in payment**: Asking an agency to set up a manual SEPA bank wire to a non-French IBAN without automated recurring billing or an established company invoice creates unnecessary administrative drag.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What proof or sample can an agency director show their client *before* signing up?
- Can an agency comfortably charge 800 € or 1,000 € / month for this deliverable to preserve healthy margins?
- What is your guarantee if a critical competitor move is missed during the month?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Irresistible Month 1 Guarantee**: Provide a strict "First month risk-reversal" (e.g. 100% refund on month 1 if the agency's client is not satisfied with the actionable quality of the signals).
2. **Show, Don't Tell**: Every cold pitch must include a real, bespoke 1-page sample audit of 2 competitors in the prospect's actual market to establish instant credibility.
3. **Turnkey Agency Client Deck**: Give the agency a 3-slide white-label sales sheet they can put in front of their clients to resell the service at 750 €+.
